About Me

Committed to thoughtful, evidence-informed care.

I am a Registered Medical Officer (RMO) under BCS (Health), working at Upazila Health Complex, Bhangura, Pabna, Bangladesh. My training includes MBBS and a Diploma in Medical Ultrasound (DMU).

My professional interests include Internal Medicine, Cardiology and Diagnostic Ultrasonography. I value continuous learning, careful clinical assessment and clear communication with patients.

This website serves as my professional profile and a place to share medical learning resources and updates.
